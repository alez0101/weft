This wiki is for Weft's admins: how to look after Weft, and the technical specification of how it is built. Only admins can see this channel, and every admin is in it; membership follows the **Admin** switch in **Settings**.

The pages are kept in the Weft repo (`docs/admin/`) and published from there, so they match the version that is running. You can add your own pages under them; those stay yours and are never overwritten.
