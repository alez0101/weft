`tests/run.sh` rebuilds the separate test stack from nothing, runs seven suites and removes it. On 2026-11-14 it passed 832 checks.

| Suite | Checks | What it proves |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Permissions (`tests/permissions.py`) | 641 | every person against every table and action, the tricky cases, accounts and channels, sub-pages and replies, the wikis and publishing, widget layouts and file bytes, search, tasks (viewers read only, assignees in the space, moves and deletes follow the list), boards (columns follow the list's space, viewers read only, a card only in its own board's columns, assignees tick off but cannot move cards, at most 10 columns, deleting a column keeps its cards), polls (viewers vote, one vote each and only their own, closed polls and voted-for choices stay put, moves and deletes follow the poll), forms (viewers answer, one answer each and only their own, answers checked against the questions, only the respondent and editors read an answer, never in the summary, search or audit, questions lock once answered, closed forms stay put, moves and deletes follow the form), links (viewers read only, only http and https addresses, tags tidied by the database, authorship and the list fixed, moves and deletes follow the list), announcements (channels only, viewers read only, end times checked, authorship and the channel fixed, membership and channel deletes followed), meetings (viewers read only, the event and task list in the meeting's own space, action items are tasks made as the caller, assignees tick theirs off, authorship fixed, moves of the meeting, its list or its event clear cross-space links, deleting a meeting keeps its tasks), RSVPs (viewers answer, one answer each and only their own, nobody answers for someone else, the event stays editors' only, ended events' answers fixed, a move keeps only answers from members of the new space, leaving a space removes your answers there, deleting the event deletes them), bookings (channels only, viewers read only, no double booking even when the trigger is bypassed, outsiders get one refusal whatever the slot, only the booker or an owner changes or cancels, only owners delete a thing, ended bookings fixed, authorship, thing and channel fixed, membership and channel deletes followed), shifts (channels only, viewers sign up only themselves and read only people off, ended shifts fixed, authorship, person, shift and channel fixed, leaving a channel takes you off its shifts, deletes follow the shift and the channel), contacts (viewers read only, | Live (`services/svc/test/live.mjs`) | 25 | notices reach members only, carry no content, follow membership changes; bad, expired and disabled tokens get nothing |
| Accounts (`services/svc/test/accounts.mjs`) | 43 | links are admin-only, single-use and expire; passwords, lockouts, session rotation and replay, sign-out, password changes, disabled accounts |
| Assistant (`services/assistant/test/assistant.mjs`) | 109 | with a stand-in model that records every prompt: restricted content never reaches the model for someone who cannot see it, failover, proposals, meaning search, files, personal keys (which key each call used), search by meaning |
| Collab (`services/collab/test/collab.mjs`) | 38 | refusals (files, task lists, polls, forms, link lists, meetings, address books), read-only viewers, live co-editing, sessions closed on losing access |
| Files (`services/files/test/files.mjs`) | 30 | who may upload and download, text from PDF / Word / text, types read from the bytes, risky types never open in place, size limits, deleting |
| Connector (`services/mcp/test/mcp.mjs`) | 68 | the AI-app connector: discovery, registration only for allowed addresses, consent requests checked, single-use codes with PKCE, read-only connections get no writing tools, tools see exactly what the person sees (with a positive control), writes follow the person's role, page edits through live co-editing, refresh rotation and replay, disconnecting, disabled accounts |

The assistant suite uses stand-ins for both the model and the embedder, so tests never call Anthropic or any real model server.

Browser checks (`tests/*-browser.cjs`, `tests/two-browsers.cjs`) drive real browsers against the test stack (`tests/run.sh --keep`): chat, documents, the calendar, proposals, whiteboards, tables, people, the wikis, signing in, files and widgets (`tests/files-widgets-browser.cjs`), search (`tests/search-browser.cjs`), tasks with the calendar and the Home widget (`tests/tasks-browser.cjs`), boards with cards dragged and moved by keyboard live, Done kept in step with Tasks, columns added, renamed, moved and deleted, and a viewer's read-only board (`tests/boards-browser.cjs`), polls with live votes, a viewer voting, closing and the Home widget (`tests/polls-browser.cjs`), forms with live answers, viewers answering but not seeing each other's answers, locked questions, CSV, copying to Sheets, closing and the Home widget (`tests/forms-browser.cjs`), links added and changed live, tag and word filters, a refused javascript: address, a viewer's read-only list and the Home widget (`tests/links-browser.cjs`), announcements posted, edited, ended and shown again live, pinned in the channel's chat, a refused past end time, a viewer's read-only view and the Home widget (`tests/announcements-browser.cjs`), meetings written live by two people without overwriting each other, linked to an event, action items added as tasks, a viewer reading and ticking off their own item, the Calendar's link back and the Home widget (`tests/meetings-browser.cjs`), RSVPs answered, changed and cleared live by two people, filters, the same answers on the event in Calendar, a viewer answering for herself and the Home widget of upcoming yeses (`tests/rsvps-browser.cjs`), bookings made, changed and cancelled live by two people, a refused overlapping slot with the database's answer, the day strip, the bookings on the Calendar opening Bookings, a viewer's read-only view and the Home widget (`tests/bookings-browser.cjs`), shifts added, claimed, filled and given back live by three people, a refused lowering of places with the database's answer, the Still open and Your shifts filters, a viewer signing herself up with no way to manage shifts, an editor taking someone off and the Home widget (`tests/shifts-browser.cjs`), contacts added and changed live, sorted by name, found by any field and by phone digits, a bad email refused with the database's answer, tel: and mailto: links, Weft search finding a book by a contact, a viewer's read-only view with a live delete and the Home widget (`tests/contacts-browser.cjs`), the connector's consent screen and Connected apps (`tests/connect-browser.cjs`) (`tests/accounts-browser.cjs`, on the public door at 3210). Run them from a folder where Playwright is installed (`npm install playwright`).
