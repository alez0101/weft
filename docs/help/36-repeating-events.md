A **repeating event** is a Calendar event that comes back every day, every week or every month until a day you choose. Enter the weekly meeting or the Tuesday rehearsal once instead of once per week.

### Making an event repeat

Open **Calendar** and drag across the time of the first one, as for any event. In the form, next to the title, space and location:

- **Repeat**: Does not repeat, Every day, Every week or Every month.
- **Until (last day)**: the last day it can fall on. Weft suggests one; change it to what you need. It can be at most a year after the first one.

Press **Add event**. Every occurrence appears on the calendar at once, at the same local time, also after the clocks change. An event every month on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months.

The panel of a repeating event says how it repeats, for example "Repeats every week until Wed, Dec 9, 2026". Each occurrence is its own event: people answer Going, Maybe or Not going for each one, and each can have its own meeting notes.

### Changing or deleting: this one or all of them

- **Change** lets you rename it or change the place. Then press **Save this one** to change only the occurrence you opened, or **Save all of them** to change every one in the series.
- **Drag** an occurrence to another time and Weft asks: **Just this one**, or **All of them** (every occurrence moves by as much, keeping its own name and place), or **Cancel**.
- **Delete this one** removes only that day's; **Delete all of them** removes every occurrence, past and coming.

"All of them" means the whole series, including the ones already past. An occurrence changed on its own stays in the series, so a later "all of them" changes it too.

### Who can see what

The same as any event: everyone in the event's space sees every occurrence and how it repeats, viewers included; nobody else does. Editors and owners make events repeat and change or delete them; viewers read them and answer for themselves. Weft (the assistant) can read repeating events and change a single one, but cannot make a series or change or delete all of them at once.
