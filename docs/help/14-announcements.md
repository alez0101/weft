title: Announcements

summary: Notices pinned to a channel, shown at the top of its chat and on Home until they end.

**Announcements** are short notices for everyone in a channel: the gates open early, the car park is closed, the budget is frozen until Friday. A notice stays at the top of the channel's chat and in everyone's **Announcements** widget on Home until the time you choose, then it goes away by itself.

### Posting an announcement

Open **Announcements** on Home and pick a channel on the left. The number next to a channel is how many notices are showing there. Press **New announcement** and fill in:

- **Title**: the one line people will read first (up to 120 characters).
- **Text**: details, if you need them (up to 1000 characters).
- **Show until**: when it stops showing. It starts one week from now; you can choose any time up to a year ahead.

Press **Post**. Everyone in the channel sees it at once.

On a notice, **Edit** changes its title, text or end time, **End now** takes it down straight away, and **Delete** removes it for good. Ended notices stay listed under **Ended** in the app, where **Show for a week** puts one back up.

### Where announcements show

- At the top of the channel's chat, the two newest notices, with a link to the rest in the app.
- In the **Announcements** widget on Home: the current notices from every channel you are in. Click one to open it in the app. If you chose your widgets before this widget existed, add it with **Customize**.
- In the app itself, current and ended notices for each channel.

Announcements are only for channels, not for your personal space or direct messages. They appear in search, and Weft answers questions from them, for the people in the channel only.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the channel sees its announcements, viewers included; nobody else can. Editors and owners of the channel post, change, end and delete them. Viewers read them but cannot change anything. Weft (the assistant) cannot post, change or delete announcements.
