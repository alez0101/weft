---
title: RSVPs
parent: Weft help
summary: Say Going, Maybe or Not going to a calendar event, and see who else is coming.
---
**RSVPs** let you tell the others whether you are coming to an event on the calendar: **Going**, **Maybe** or **Not going**. Everyone in the event's channel sees the counts and who answered what, so the organiser knows how many to expect and you can see who else will be there.

### Answering

There are two places to answer:

- **On the event in Calendar.** Click an event; under its time and place you see the counts, the three buttons and who is coming.
- **In the RSVPs app** on Home. It lists every upcoming event in the channels you are in, by day, each with the same buttons. On the left, **Not answered** shows the events you have not answered yet, and **Going** and **Maybe** show the ones you have. The numbers say how many are in each.

Press **Going**, **Maybe** or **Not going**. Press another one to change your mind, or press your answer again to clear it. You can change your answer until the event ends; after that the answers stay as they were.

### Your upcoming yeses

The **Your upcoming yeses** widget on Home lists the events you said **Going** to that have not ended yet, soonest first. Click one to open it in Calendar. If you chose your widgets before this widget existed, add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the event's channel sees its answers, with names; nobody else can. Everyone in the channel can answer, viewers included, because answering is about you, not about the event. You only ever answer for yourself: nobody, not even an owner, can answer, change or clear an answer for someone else. Only editors and owners change or delete the event itself.

If an event is moved to another channel, the answers of people who are not in that channel are removed. If you leave a channel, or are removed from it, your answers on its events are removed too. Deleting an event deletes its answers. Weft (the assistant) cannot answer for anyone, and does not yet use the answers when you ask it something.
