**Links** keeps the useful web addresses for a group in one place: the supplier's price list, the site map, the booking page. A link list belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and everyone in that space can see it.

### Making a list and adding links

Open **Links** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New link list**. Type a name over "Useful links".

Press **Add a link** and fill in:

- **Address**: the web address. If you leave out `https://`, Weft adds it. Only `http` and `https` addresses are accepted.
- **Title**: what people will see. Leave it empty to use the site's name.
- **Note**: what the link is for, or when to use it.
- **Tags**: a few words separated by commas, such as `food, budget`. Tags are saved in lower case.

Weft only keeps what you type. It never opens or downloads the page, so there are no previews, and a link that stops working is not noticed until someone clicks it.

Press **Edit** or **Delete** on a link to change or remove it. Rename, move, share or delete a whole list the same way as a document.

### Finding a link

- Click a link's title to open it in a new browser tab.
- Type in **Filter links** to show only links whose title, address, note or tags contain those words.
- Click a tag to show only links with that tag; click it again, or **Show all**, to see everything.
- The search box at the top finds link lists by the words in their links, and Weft can answer questions such as "where is the site map?" from them.
- The **New links** widget on Home lists the newest links in lists you can see. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the list's space can see its links and open them; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners add, change and delete links. Viewers can read and open them but not change anything. Weft can read link lists to answer questions, but it cannot add, change or delete links.
