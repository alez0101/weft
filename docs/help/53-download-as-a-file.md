---
title: Download as a file
parent: Weft help
summary: Save an expense list, an inventory, an address book, a form's answers or a channel's calendar as a plain file for a spreadsheet, your phone's contacts or another calendar app.
---
Some apps have a **Download** button that saves what you are looking at as an ordinary file. The file is made in your browser from what is already on your screen: nothing is sent anywhere, and it holds only what you can already see. It is part of each app; there is nothing to switch on.

### Where to find it

- **Expenses**: **Download CSV** next to the Expenses heading. One row per expense, oldest first: date, what for, amount, who paid, how it was split, and a column with each person's share. Amounts are plain numbers (12.50), so a spreadsheet can add them up.
- **Inventory**: **Download CSV** next to the filter. Every thing, with where it is kept, its note, who has it, since when and its back-by date.
- **Contacts**: **Download CSV** for a spreadsheet, or **Download vCard** (a .vcf file) to import into your phone's or computer's contacts. Both hold every contact in the book, not only the ones the filter shows.
- **Forms**: on the **Answers** tab, **Download CSV** gives one row per answer. Only editors and owners see this tab.
- **Calendar**: under **Download as a file** in the side bar, pick one of your spaces and press **Download .ics**. You get every event in that space, past and coming, to import into another calendar app. Repeating events come out as one event per day they happen.

### Good to know

- A download is a copy taken at that moment. Later changes in Weft are not in it; download again to get them. Importing the same calendar file again updates its events instead of adding them twice.
- In a CSV file, text that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` gets an apostrophe in front, so a spreadsheet shows it rather than running it as a formula.
- Once a file is on your computer, Weft no longer decides who sees it. Think before you send it on: it may hold phone numbers, amounts or answers that only your channel was meant to see.

### Who can download what

Anyone who can open the list, book or inventory can download it, viewers included, because the file holds nothing they cannot already read. Form answers download only for editors and owners of the form's channel, the same people who see the answers. The calendar offers only the spaces you are in.
