---
title: Poll and form deadlines
parent: Weft help
summary: Give a poll or a form a closing time; it closes by itself when the time comes, and the time shows on it and in the Home widgets.
---
A poll or a form can have a **closing time**. When that time comes, it closes by itself, exactly as if someone had pressed **Close poll** or **Close form**: nobody can vote, answer or change their vote or answer any more, and the votes and answers stay as they were.

### Setting a closing time

- Open the poll or form and press **Set closing time**. Pick a day and time in the **Closes at** box and press **Save time**. The time is in your own time zone; everyone else sees it in theirs.
- **Change closing time** moves it, and **No closing time** removes it.
- The time must be in the future and no more than a year ahead. If you pick a time that has passed, Weft says so and keeps the old one.

### Seeing when it closes

- The poll or form says when it closes next to its votes or answers, such as "closes Fri 20 Nov, 5:00 PM". Once the time has passed it says "closed by itself" instead, without anyone needing to reload.
- The **Polls to vote in** and **Forms to fill in** widgets on Home show the closing time, with the ones closing soonest at the top. A time less than a day away is shown in red. Polls and forms that have closed drop off the widgets.
- Weft can answer questions such as "when does the parade poll close?".

### Opening it again

Press **Reopen poll** or **Reopen form**. A poll or form that closed by itself loses its closing time when you reopen it; you can then set a new one. Setting a later closing time on a poll or form that closed by itself also opens it again, until that time.

### Who can do what

Everyone in the poll's or form's space sees the closing time; nobody else does. Editors and owners of the space set, change and remove it, the same people who can close and reopen it. Viewers see it but cannot change it. Weft can read the closing time to answer questions, but it cannot change it.
