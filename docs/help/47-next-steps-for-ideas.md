---
title: Next steps for ideas
parent: Weft help
summary: When an idea is planned, add it as a task in one press; when it is declined, say why in a line shown under it.
---
When the owners of a space change the status of an idea in **Ideas**, Weft offers the obvious next step. A **Planned** idea can become a task in one of the channel's task lists, so the work actually gets done. A **Declined** idea can carry a short reason, so the person who suggested it knows why.

### Adding a planned idea as a task

1. Open the ideas board and choose **Planned** in the menu next to the idea. The status is saved at once.
2. Under the idea, Weft asks which list to add it to. Only task lists in the same channel as the board are offered. Pick one and press **Add task**.
3. The idea now shows **Task open in** and the list's name. Press the name to open the list.

The task is an ordinary task, titled like the idea: you can give it to someone, set a due date or tick it off in **Tasks**, and it shows on Boards, the Calendar and Home like any other. When someone ticks it off, the idea shows **Task done**.

Press **Not now** to skip; the idea keeps an **Add as a task** button for later. An idea becomes a task only once. If the task is deleted, or the list or the board moves to another channel, the idea simply stops showing a task.

If the channel has no task list yet, make one in **Tasks** first.

### Saying why an idea was declined

1. Choose **Declined** in the menu next to the idea. The status is saved at once, and the idea stays in view while Weft asks why.
2. Type a short reason (up to 300 characters, one line), such as "The fire service said no for this site", and press **Save reason**. Press **Skip** if you would rather not say.
3. The reason shows under the idea, after **Declined:**. **Change reason** (or **Add a reason**) lets you change it later.

If the idea is taken out of Declined again, its reason goes.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the board's channel, viewers included, sees an idea's task and its reason for being declined; nobody else does, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Only owners of the channel add an idea as a task or give a reason, the same people who set the status. Weft can read the reasons and whether the task is done to answer questions, but it cannot change them.
