---
title: Bookings
parent: Weft help
summary: Book a channel's shared things (a room, a van, a projector) for a time slot, with no double booking.
---
**Bookings** keep track of the things a channel shares, like a meeting room, a van or a projector, and who has them when. A time slot can only be booked once: if someone already has the van from 10:00 to 12:00, nobody else can book it for any part of that time.

### Adding a thing to book

Open **Bookings** on Home and press **New thing to book**. Give it a name, choose the channel it belongs to, and add a note if it helps (where it is, who has the key). Only channels have things to book, and you need to be an editor or owner of the channel.

### Booking

Pick the thing on the left. The strip at the top shows one day, from 07:00 to 22:00, with the slots already booked; use the arrows, **Today** or the date to look at another day. Below it, **Coming up** lists every booking that has not ended, by day, with who booked it.

Press **Book** and choose the day, the start and the end (press **Ends on another day** for a booking that runs overnight or for several days), and add a note if you like. If the time overlaps another booking, Weft says so and nothing is booked; pick another time. A slot that starts exactly when another ends is fine. A booking can last up to 31 days.

To move or shorten your booking, press **Change**; to give the slot back, press **Cancel booking**. Once a booking has ended it stays as it was.

### On the Calendar and Home

Bookings show on the **Calendar** as "Van: Olive", in the colour of their channel. Untick **Bookings** there to hide them. Click one to open it in Bookings (you change bookings there, not on the Calendar).

The **Your bookings** widget on Home lists your own bookings that have not ended, soonest first. Click one to open it. If you chose your widgets before this widget existed, add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the channel sees its things and every booking, with names; nobody else can. Editors and owners add things, rename them and book them. You change or cancel your own bookings; an owner of the channel can change or cancel anyone's. Only owners delete a thing, and its bookings go with it. Viewers can see when something is free but cannot book it; ask an owner to make you an editor.

Things and bookings never move to another channel. Deleting a channel deletes its things and their bookings. If you leave a channel, bookings you made there stay until an owner cancels them. Weft (the assistant) can read bookings but cannot book, change or cancel anything. Things to book, with their bookings from the last 30 days on, appear in search for the people in the channel.
