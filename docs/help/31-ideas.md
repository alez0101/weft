**Ideas** is a board where a group collects suggestions: a water refill point by the main stage, bike racks by gate B, a quiet tent for families. Everyone votes for the ones they want, so the popular ideas rise to the top, and the owners of the space mark each one **New**, **Planned**, **Done** or **Declined**, so people can see what happened to their suggestion. An ideas board belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and only the people in that space can see it.

### Making a board and suggesting ideas

Open **Ideas** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New ideas board**. Type a name over "Ideas", such as "Festival ideas". A channel can have more than one board.

Press **Suggest an idea**, write the idea in one line, add details if they help (why it matters, what it would take), and press **Add idea**. A new idea is **New**.

### Voting

Press the arrow next to an idea to vote for it; press it again to take your vote back. You have one vote per idea, and you may vote for your own. Votes are open while an idea is new or planned. Once it is done or declined, voting is closed, but the votes it got stay on it.

### Status

The owners of the space choose the status from the menu next to each idea: **New** (not looked at yet), **Planned** (we will do it), **Done** or **Declined** (we will not do it). Editors and viewers see the status but cannot change it.

### Changing and tidying up

- **Edit** changes your own idea; nobody else can reword it, not even an owner.
- Editors and owners can **Delete** any idea, to keep the board tidy. Its votes go with it.
- Rename, move, share or delete a whole board the same way as a document. Moving it to another channel takes all its ideas and votes with it.

### Finding ideas

- The buttons at the top show **Open** ideas (new and planned, the default), only **New**, **Planned**, **Done** or **Declined**, or **All**, each with how many there are.
- **Sort by** most votes (the default) or newest first.
- Type in the find box to show only ideas whose title or details contain those words.
- The search box at the top finds boards by the words in their ideas.
- **Ask Weft**: Weft reads the boards you can see, with each idea's status and votes, and answers from them with a link to the board.
- The **Top ideas** widget on Home lists the open ideas with the most votes on boards you can see. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the board's space can see its ideas, who suggested each one, its status and who voted for it; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners suggest ideas. Viewers read everything and can vote (like voting in a poll), but cannot suggest, change or delete anything. Only owners set the status. Weft can read boards to answer questions, but it cannot suggest, vote, change a status or delete.
