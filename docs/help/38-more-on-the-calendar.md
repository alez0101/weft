The **Calendar** shows more than events. Besides the events of your spaces, it can show:

- **Tasks due**: open tasks with a due date, as all-day entries ("Task: …").
- **Milestones**: the milestones on a channel's Timeline, as all-day entries ("Milestone: …").
- **Your shifts**: the shifts you signed up for in Shifts, at their times ("Shift: …"). Shifts you are not on stay in Shifts.
- **Bookings**: bookings of shared things ("Van: Olive").
- **Time off**: who is away, as all-day entries ("Away: …").

Each entry has the colour of its space, and the space switches at the top of the left side hide a space's entries along with its events.

### Switching them on and off

Under **Also show** on the left, untick a kind to hide it and tick it to show it again. Weft remembers your choice, so the Calendar looks the same the next time you open it, on any computer. Your choice only changes your own Calendar.

### Changing them

These entries are read-only on the Calendar: click one to open where it lives. A milestone opens that channel's **Timeline**, a shift opens **Shifts**, a task opens its list, a booking opens **Bookings** and time off opens **Time off**. Change them there.

### Who can see what

The same as in each app: you see a channel's milestones, bookings and time off only if you are a member of the channel, viewers included, and only your own shifts. Nobody else sees which kinds you switched off, and Weft (the assistant) does not read it.
