**Inventory** is a shared list of the things your group owns: tents, tools, the projector, the van keys. For each thing it says where it is kept and who has it right now. When someone takes a thing, **check it out** to them; when it comes back, **check it in**. Weft keeps a record of every check out and check in, so you can see who had what and when. An inventory belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and only the people in that space can see it.

### Making an inventory

Open **Inventory** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New inventory**. Type a name over "Our things", such as "Festival gear". Rename, move, share or delete a whole inventory the same way as a document.

### Adding things

Press **Add a thing** and fill in:

- **Thing**: what it is, such as "Tent" or "Extension lead".
- **Kept in**: where it lives when nobody has it, such as "Shed, shelf 2". Places you have used before are offered as you type. You can leave it empty.
- **Note**: anything worth knowing, such as the size or what goes with it.

Things are shown grouped by where they are kept. **Edit** changes a thing and **Delete** removes it with its record.

### Checking out and checking in

- A thing that nobody has says **In**. Press **Check out**, choose who is taking it (it starts as you) and press **Check out** again. It now says who has it and since when.
- When it comes back, press **Check in**. It says **In** again.
- If you are a viewer in the space, you can still borrow things: press **Check out to me** on a thing that is in, and **Check in** when you bring it back. You cannot check things out to other people or check in what someone else has.
- If two people press **Check out** on the same thing at the same time, the second is told that someone has just checked it out.

Beside the list, **Checked out now** shows everything that is out and who has it, and **Recent check outs and check ins** shows the latest changes. **History** under a thing shows its own record. Everyone looking sees changes at once.

Someone who leaves the space still shows as having what they took, so nothing goes missing from the record. An editor can check it in when it comes back.

### Finding things

- Type in the box above the list to find a thing by its name, where it is kept, its note or who has it. **In**, **Checked out** and **With you** narrow the list.
- The **Things you have** widget on Home lists what is checked out to you, in every inventory. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.
- The search box at the top finds inventories by the things in them, and Weft can answer questions such as "who has the gazebo?" or "where is the extension lead kept?".

### Who can see what

Everyone in the inventory's space can see its things, who has them and the record; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners add, change and delete things, and check things out to or in from anyone in the space. Viewers can check out things that are in to themselves and check in what they have, and nothing more. Weft can read inventories to answer questions, but it cannot add, change or check out anything.
