---
**Expenses** keeps a shared record of money spent for the group: the van, the groceries, the tickets. For each expense you write down what it was for, how much, who paid and who it was for. Weft keeps everyone's running balance and works out who should pay whom to make everyone even. No money moves through Weft: it is only the record. An expense list belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and only the people in that space can see it.

### Making a list

Open **Expenses** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New expense list**. Type a name over "Shared costs", such as "Festival costs". Rename, move, share or delete a whole list the same way as a document.

### Adding an expense

Press **Add an expense** and fill in:

- **What for** and the **Amount** (such as 41.30; a comma works too). There is no currency: use one currency per list.
- **Paid by**: who paid. It starts as you.
- **Date**: when it was spent. It starts as today.
- **Split**: **Evenly** shares it out equally between the people ticked under **Between**. If it does not divide exactly, the odd cents go to the first people in the list. **By amounts** lets you type each person's share; **Add expense** stays off until the shares add up to the amount, and the form tells you how much is still to share out.

Everyone in the list's space can be chosen. **Edit** changes an expense and **Delete** removes it; the balances follow at once, for everyone looking.

### Balances and settling up

Beside the expenses, **Balances** shows each person's total paid, their share and the difference: who **is owed** money and who **owes** it. **To settle up** lists the fewest payments that would make everyone even.

When someone pays someone back, write it down as an expense too: paid by the person who paid back, split **by amounts** to the person they paid. For example, if Pat gives Olive 25.00, add "Pat paid Olive back", 25.00, paid by Pat, by amounts: Olive 25.00. Their balances move towards even.

Someone who leaves the space stays in the expenses they were part of, so the record and the balances stay right.

### Finding things

- The **Your balances** widget on Home shows what you owe or are owed in each list where you are not even. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.
- The search box at the top finds lists by the words in their expenses, and Weft can answer questions such as "who owes whom for the festival?" or "what did we spend on the van?" from them.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the list's space can see its expenses and balances; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners add, change and delete expenses. Viewers can read everything but not change it. Weft can read expense lists to answer questions, but it cannot add or change anything.
