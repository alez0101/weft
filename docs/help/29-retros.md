title: Retros
parent: Weft help
summary: Look back on a period together: what went well, what to improve and what to do next, with notes you can post anonymously and actions that become tasks.
---
**Retros** is for looking back after a stretch of work, an event or a season: what went well, what to improve, and what you will do about it. Everyone in the channel writes notes, and anyone can post a note **anonymously** if they would rather not put their name to it. Actions can be turned into real tasks, so they do not get lost once the retro is over. A retro belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and only the people in that space can see it.

### Making a retro

Open **Retros** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New retro**. Type a name over "Retro", such as "Festival 2026" or "Sprint 12". Editors and owners can then set the **Period** the retro looks back on and choose the task list that **Actions become tasks in** (a list in the same space; make one in **Tasks** first if there is none).

### Writing notes

A retro has three columns: **Went well**, **To improve** and **Actions**. Type in the box at the top of a column and press **Add** (or Ctrl+Enter). Tick **Post anonymously** first if you do not want your name on the note.

- Your own notes say "(you)". Press **Change** or **Delete** on them to fix or remove them. Nobody else can change your note.
- Editors and owners can delete any note, for tidying up, but they cannot change what someone else wrote.
- A note stays in the column you put it in, and whether it is anonymous is fixed once it is posted. To change either, delete the note and write it again.
- Everyone looking at the retro sees new notes at once.

### Anonymous notes

An anonymous note shows **Anonymous** to everyone, owners and admins included. Weft keeps who wrote it only so that you can still change or delete your own note; nobody can look it up in Weft, it is not in the change history, and Weft's assistant cannot see it either. Your name is still not hidden from the people in the room in other ways: they may guess from when a note appeared or from how it is written.

### Turning actions into tasks

Editors and owners see **Make a task** on each action once a task list is chosen. Pick who it is for and a due date (both optional) and press **Make the task**. The task appears in the chosen list, and in Tasks, Boards, the Calendar and on Home like any other task. The action then shows the task: who it is for, when it is due, and a tick once it is done. The person it is assigned to can tick it off from the retro.

### Finding retros

- The search box at the top finds retros by the words in their notes, and Weft can answer questions such as "what did we say about the bins?" from them. It never says who wrote a note.
- The **Open retro actions** widget on Home lists actions from the last 60 days that are not a task yet or whose task is not done. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the retro's space can see it and add notes, viewers too: a retro is for everyone to take part in. Nobody outside the space can see it, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners also set the period and the task list, delete any note and turn actions into tasks. Rename, move, share or delete a whole retro the same way as a document; moving it to another channel takes its notes with it, but not its task list or the links to tasks in the old channel (the tasks stay where they are). Weft can read retros to answer questions, but it cannot change them.
