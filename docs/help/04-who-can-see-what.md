---
title: Who can see what
parent: Weft help
summary: Spaces, roles and sharing.
---
Everything in Weft lives in a **space**: a channel, a direct message, your personal space, or your Weft conversation. You see something only if you are a member of its space. The same rule applies to chat, documents, the calendar, search and the assistant.

### Roles in a space

- **Viewer**: reads everything in the space and can chat.
- **Editor**: also creates and changes documents, wiki pages and events.
- **Owner**: also manages the space and its members.

One exception: the answers to a **form** are seen only by the person who gave them and by the editors and owners of the form's space.

### Sharing

Sharing a document or event into a channel posts a link. **A link gives no access**: people who are not in the document's space get a "You don't have access" page. Weft reminds you of this when you share.

### Moving and removing

- **Move to** puts a document in another space you can edit. Only that space's members can open it afterwards, and anyone who had it open and can no longer see it is disconnected.
- **Delete** asks you to confirm first. Sub-pages of a deleted wiki page move to the top level.
- When someone is removed from a space, their open documents in it close immediately.
