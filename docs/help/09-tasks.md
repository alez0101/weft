**Tasks** keeps to-do lists. A list belongs to a space (your personal space or a channel), and each task in it has a title, the person it is assigned to, a due date, and whether it is done.

### Making a list

Open **Tasks** on Home. Choose the space at the top, press **New task list**, and type a name over "New task list". Rename, move, share or delete a list the same way as a document.

### Adding and changing tasks

- Type the task in **Add a task**, choose who it is for and when it is due (both optional), and press **Add task**.
- Tick the box to mark a task done; untick it to reopen it. Done tasks move to the bottom; **Show done** hides them and **Delete done tasks** clears them.
- Click a task's title to change it, or change who it is for and its due date in place. Tasks that are past their due date are marked in red.
- **Only mine** shows just the tasks assigned to you.
- A task can only be assigned to someone in the list's space. If you move a list to another space, tasks assigned to people who are not in the new space become unassigned.

### Due dates elsewhere in Weft

- The **Calendar** shows open tasks with a due date as all-day entries, marked "Task:". Click one to open its list. The **Tasks due** switch on the left hides them.
- The **Tasks due soon** widget on Home lists open tasks that are overdue or due in the next seven days, yours first. If you have customized your widgets before, add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the list's space can see the list and its tasks; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners of the space can add, change, tick off and delete tasks; viewers can read them but not change them, even a task assigned to them. Weft can read your lists and answer questions such as "what is due this week in #festival?", but it cannot change or delete tasks.
