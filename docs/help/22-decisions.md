**Decisions** is a log of what a group has decided: gates open at 9:30, no single-use cups, volunteers park behind the school. Each decision keeps the reason with it, so months later anyone can find out why something is the way it is, and whether it still holds. A decision log belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and only the people in that space can see it.

### Making a log and writing decisions down

Open **Decisions** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New decision log**. Type a name over "Decisions", such as "Site decisions" or "Budget". A channel can have more than one.

Press **Add a decision** and fill in:

- **Decision**: what was decided, in one line. The only thing you have to fill in.
- **Context**: why, and what else was considered. This is the part people will look for later.
- **Decided by**: a person, a group or a meeting, such as "Organizers meeting" or "Volunteer vote".
- **Date**: when it was decided. It starts as today; change it if you are writing down an older decision.
- **Replaces**: if this decision changes an earlier one in the same log, pick it here.

Press **Edit** or **Delete** on a decision to change or remove it. Rename, move, share or delete a whole log the same way as a document. Moving it to another channel moves all its decisions with it.

### When a decision changes

Don't edit the old decision to say something new: add a new decision and pick the old one under **Replaces**. The old one stays in the log, crossed out and marked **Replaced**, with a link to the one that replaced it, and the new one links back. That way the history of why things changed stays readable.

A decision can only replace one in the same log, and each decision can be replaced only once. Weft refuses a loop (A replaces B, and B replaces A) and says why above the list. Deleting a decision that was replaced removes the link from the newer one.

### Finding a decision

- The newest decisions are at the top, by the date they were decided.
- Type in the find box to show only decisions whose text, context or "decided by" contain those words.
- Tick **Hide replaced** to see only the decisions that are still in force.
- The search box at the top finds decision logs by the words in their decisions, and Weft can answer questions such as "why do the gates open at 9:30?" from them.
- The **Recent decisions** widget on Home lists the most recently decided decisions in logs you can see. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the log's space can see its decisions; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners add, change and delete decisions. Viewers can read them and follow the links between them, but not change anything. Weft can read decision logs to answer questions, but it cannot add, change or delete decisions.
