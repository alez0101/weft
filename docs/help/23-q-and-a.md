**Q and A** is a board of questions and answers for a group: where do volunteers park, who signs the sponsor contract, is there a cloakroom. People upvote the answers that helped, and the person who asked marks the one that answered it. Next time someone wonders, they find it there, or simply ask Weft. A Q and A board belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and only the people in that space can see it.

### Making a board and asking

Open **Q and A** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New Q and A board**. Type a name over "Questions", such as "Volunteer questions". A channel can have more than one board.

Press **Ask a question**, write the question in one line, add details if they help, and press **Ask**. The newest questions are at the top.

### Answering, upvoting and accepting

- Write in the box under a question and press **Answer**. A question can have many answers.
- Press the arrow next to an answer to **upvote** it; press it again to take your upvote back. You can upvote each answer once, and not your own.
- The person who asked, or an owner of the space, presses **Accept** on the answer that settled it. That answer moves to the top, is marked **Accepted answer**, and the question shows **Answered**. Press **Unaccept** to take the mark off, or **Accept** on another answer to move it. Other answers are listed by upvotes.
- **Edit** changes your own question or answer; nobody else can reword what you wrote, not even an owner. Editors and owners can **Delete** any question or answer, to keep the board tidy. Deleting a question deletes its answers.

Rename, move, share or delete a whole board the same way as a document. Moving it to another channel takes all its questions, answers and upvotes with it.

### Finding an answer

- Type in the find box to show only questions whose question, details or answers contain those words.
- Tick **No accepted answer yet** to see what still needs an answer.
- The search box at the top finds boards by the words in their questions and answers.
- **Ask Weft**: Weft reads the boards you can see and answers from them, preferring the accepted answer, then the most upvoted, with a link to the board.
- The **Open questions** widget on Home lists the newest questions without an accepted answer on boards you can see. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the board's space can see its questions, answers and who wrote them, and how many upvotes each answer has; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners ask and answer. Viewers read everything and can upvote answers (like voting in a poll), but cannot ask, answer or change anything. Weft can read boards to answer questions, but it cannot ask, answer, upvote or delete.
