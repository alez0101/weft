### Make a channel

Press **+** next to **Channels** in the chat rail, give it a name and press **Create**. You become its owner, and its people panel opens so you can add people straight away.

### See and manage who is in a channel

Open the channel and press the member count under its name ("3 members, see people").

- **Everyone** sees who is in the channel and their role.
- **Owners** can add people, change roles, remove people, and rename or delete the channel.
- **Anyone** can leave a channel, except the help channel, which everyone can read.

### Roles

- **Owner**: manages the channel and its people.
- **Editor**: writes documents, wiki pages, tables, whiteboards and events in it.
- **Viewer**: reads and chats.

A channel always keeps an owner: the last owner cannot leave or change their own role until someone else is owner. Removing someone takes away their access at once, including any document they have open.

### Adding someone new to Weft

An admin makes an invite link for their email in **Settings**, **People**, and sends it to them. They open it, choose their own password, and their account is created with their own personal space and Weft conversation. After that they appear in the **Add a person** list, and channel owners can add them.

Forgot your password? Ask an admin for a reset link.

### Deleting a channel

Deleting a channel deletes its messages, documents, wiki pages, tables, whiteboards and events for everyone. Weft asks you to type the channel's name first. It cannot be undone.

### For admins: accounts

**Settings** lists everyone who has signed in, with their channels and last sign-in.

- **Admin**: lets someone open Settings. It does not let them see spaces they are not in.
- **Invite** and **Reset link**: make single-use links for a new person or a forgotten password.
- **Disable**: signs the person out everywhere at once and stops them signing in or seeing anything. Their channels are kept, so **Enable** restores everything.
- You cannot change your own account there, so Weft always keeps at least one admin.
