### Where to ask

- **In your Weft conversation** (top of the chat rail): Weft uses everything you can open, across all your channels, documents and events. Only you see this conversation.
- **In a channel**: start your message with **@Weft**. Weft only uses that channel's messages, documents and events, because everyone in the channel reads the answer. It can only post to, and add events in, that channel.

### What you get back

- A short answer with numbered links. Click a link to see where the answer came from.
- For actions, a card with **Confirm** and **Dismiss**.
- A note under the answer saying which model wrote it.

### Examples

- "@Weft is the schedule locked for Saturday?"
- "@Weft who asked about the 13:00 set at P04?"
- "@Weft schedule a porch captain walkthrough Wednesday at 10am."
- "@Weft move The Backyard Radio to the garage porch."
- In your Weft conversation: "Post in #volunteers that check-in moves to 11:30."
- "What can you do?"

### Tips

- Ask in your Weft conversation when the answer may be in a channel others in the room cannot see.
- Give dates and times the way you would say them; Weft uses your time zone.
- If Weft says it couldn't find something, it may be in a space you are not in. Ask someone who is.
