**Polls** asks a quick question and counts the answers. A poll belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space). Everyone in that space can vote once, and everyone sees the results change as people vote.

### Making a poll

Open **Polls** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New poll**. Type your question over "New poll". A new poll starts with the choices Yes and No; press **Edit choices** to rename them, remove them or add more, then **Done editing**.

To ask a channel, make the poll in that channel and use **Share to a channel** to post a link to it in the chat. Rename, move or delete a poll the same way as a document.

### Voting

- Pick a choice to vote. Pick another to change your vote, or press **Withdraw my vote** to take it back.
- Each choice shows its votes, its share of all votes, and who voted for it. Your own vote is outlined.
- The **Polls to vote in** widget on Home lists open polls you have not voted in yet. If you have customized your widgets before, add it with **Customize**.

### Closing a poll

An editor or owner of the space can press **Close poll** when the vote is over. Nobody can vote or change their vote in a closed poll, and the leading choice is marked. **Reopen poll** opens it again.

Once someone has voted for a choice, it cannot be renamed or removed, so nobody's vote ends up meaning something else. Choices without votes can still be changed while the poll is open.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the poll's space can see the poll, its votes and who voted for what; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Polls are not anonymous. Everyone in the space can vote, viewers included, and can change only their own vote. Editors and owners make polls, change their choices, and close or reopen them. Weft can read a poll's question and vote counts and answer questions such as "which day is winning the parade poll?", but it cannot vote or change a poll.
