---
**Shifts** is for rotas and volunteer slots: the bar from 18:00 to 22:00 needs three people, the gate on Saturday morning needs one. A channel's editors set up the shifts; everyone in the channel signs themselves up.

### Signing up

Open **Shifts** on Home. Upcoming shifts from all your channels are listed by day. Each one shows its time, its channel, who is on it and how many places are still open (filled dots are taken places, your own is green).

Press **Take a place** to sign up. To drop out, press **Give back my place**, so someone else can take it. A full shift takes nobody else; if two people press for the last place at the same moment, only one gets it and the other is told the shift is full.

On the left, **Still open** shows only shifts with free places, and **Your shifts** shows the ones you are on. If more than one channel has shifts, you can pick a channel there too.

### Setting up shifts

If you are an editor or owner of a channel, press **New shift**. Choose the channel, give the shift a name, a day, a start and end time (an end before the start means it ends the next day, for night shifts) and how many people it needs (1 to 200). Add a note if it helps: where to meet, what to bring. A shift can last up to 7 days.

Press **Change** to move a shift or change its places. You cannot give a shift fewer places than the people already on it; take someone off first. To take someone off a shift, press the × next to their name. **Delete** removes the shift and its sign-ups.

Once a shift has ended it stays as it was: nobody signs up, gives back or is taken off, and it cannot be changed.

### On Home

The **Your shifts** widget lists the shifts you are on that have not ended, soonest first, with how full each one is. Click one to open Shifts. If you chose your widgets before this widget existed, add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the channel sees its shifts and who is on each one; nobody else can. Every member signs up, viewers included, but only themselves. Editors and owners add, change and delete shifts and can take anyone off. Shifts never move to another channel. If you leave a channel, or are removed from it, you come off its shifts. Deleting a channel deletes its shifts. Weft (the assistant) can read shifts but cannot sign anyone up or change anything, and shifts are not in search.
