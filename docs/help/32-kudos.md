**Kudos** is a wall of thank-yous for a channel: someone carried the chairs back in the rain, fixed the sound desk just in time, or handed out every map. Write it down so they, and everyone else in the channel, can see it.

### Giving kudos

Open **Kudos** on Home and pick a channel on the left. At the top of the wall:

- **For**: who you are thanking. You can choose anyone else in the channel.
- **Thank you for**: what they did and why it helped (up to 500 characters).

Press **Give kudos**. It goes to the top of the wall, and everyone in the channel sees it at once.

On your own kudos, **Edit** changes the words (it then says *edited*) and **Delete** removes it. You cannot change who it is for; delete it and give a new one instead. Owners of the channel can also delete anyone's kudos, to tidy up.

### Where kudos show

- In the app, one wall per channel, newest first. A kudos for you is marked **you**.
- In the **Recent kudos** widget on Home: the newest kudos from every channel you are in. Click one to open that channel's wall. It is not on by default; add it with **Customize**.

Kudos are only for channels, not for your personal space or direct messages. They appear in search, and Weft answers questions from them, for the people in the channel only.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the channel sees its kudos, viewers included; nobody else can. Editors and owners of the channel give kudos, to other members of the channel only, never to themselves. Viewers read the wall but cannot give, change or delete kudos. If someone later leaves the channel, the kudos for them stay on the wall. Weft (the assistant) cannot give, change or delete kudos.
