---
title: Timeline
parent: Weft help
summary: The milestones of a channel on one line, in date order, with today marked and past ones greyed.
---
**Timeline** shows the milestones of a channel on one horizontal line: the site visit, the day the line-up is announced, the stage build, the day the gates open. Today is marked on the line, so you can see at a glance what is behind you and what is coming.

### Adding milestones

Open **Timeline** on Home and pick a channel on the left. Below the line:

- **Milestone**: what happens, in a few words (up to 120 characters).
- **Date**: the day it happens.
- **Note**: anything worth adding (optional, up to 300 characters).

Press **Add milestone**. It takes its place on the line by date, and everyone in the channel sees it at once.

**Edit** on a milestone changes its words or its date (it then moves along the line); **Delete** removes it. Anyone who can add milestones in the channel can change or delete any of them, not only their own.

### Reading the line

- Milestones run from oldest on the left to newest on the right. Scroll sideways to see more; when you open a channel, the line starts at today.
- The **Today** marker sits between what has passed and what is coming. Milestones before today are greyed; a milestone today is highlighted.
- Each milestone says how far away it is ("in 3 weeks", "6 days ago"), and the top of the page names the next one.
- The **Next milestones** widget on Home lists the coming milestones in all your channels, soonest first. Click one to open that channel's timeline. It is not on by default; add it with **Customize**.

Timelines are only for channels, not for your personal space or direct messages. Milestones do not appear in search, and Weft does not answer questions from them.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the channel sees its timeline, viewers included; nobody else can. Editors and owners of the channel add, change and delete milestones. Viewers read the timeline but cannot change it. Weft (the assistant) cannot add, change or delete milestones.
