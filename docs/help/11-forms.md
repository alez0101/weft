**Forms** asks a few questions and collects the answers in a table, for things like T-shirt sizes, who brings what, or sign-ups. A form belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space). Everyone in that space can fill it in once.

### Making a form

Open **Forms** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New form**. Type the form's name over "New form". A new form starts with one question, "Your name"; in **Edit questions** you can reword it, add more, and choose for each:

- **Short answer** (one line), **Long answer** (a few lines) or **One choice** (type the choices one per line),
- **Required**, if every answer must include it.

To ask a channel, make the form in that channel and use **Share to a channel** to post a link to it in the chat. Rename, move or delete a form the same way as a document.

### Filling it in

Open the form, answer the questions and press **Send my answer**. Questions marked * are required. You can come back and press **Update my answer**, or **Withdraw my answer** to delete it, until the form is closed. The **Forms to fill in** widget on Home lists open forms you have not answered yet. If you have customized your widgets before, add it with **Customize**.

### Collecting the answers

Editors and owners of the space press **Answers** to see every answer as a table: one row per person, one column per question, filling in as people answer. From there:

- **Download CSV** saves the table as a file for a spreadsheet program.
- **Copy to a new table** makes a table in Sheets, in the same space, with the answers so far. It is a copy: later answers are not added to it.

Once someone has answered, the existing questions cannot be reworded or removed, so no answer ends up meaning something else. You can still add new questions. **Close form** stops new answers and changes; **Reopen form** opens it again.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the form's space can see the form and its questions; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. **Answers are not public**: you see your own answer, and the editors and owners of the space see everyone's, with names. Weft can tell anyone in the space how many people have answered, and can read the answers only for someone who may see them. It cannot fill in, change or close a form.
