**Goals** keeps what a group is aiming for and how far it has got: sell out the festival, cut waste, find three sponsors. Each goal has one or more **key results**, numbers you can count ("tickets sold: 120 of 400"), and Weft works out the progress from them. A goal set belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and only the people in that space can see it.

### Making a goal set

Open **Goals** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New goal set**. Type a name over "Goals", such as "Spring 2027" or "Festival goals". A channel can have more than one, for example one per season.

Opening a set shows its progress: the **Overall** bar at the top, then each goal with its own bar and its key results underneath.

### Goals and key results

Press **Add a goal** and give it a title. A note (why it matters, who is on it) and a due date are optional. A goal past its due date that is not reached yet says **Overdue**.

Under a goal, press **Add a key result** and fill in:

- **Key result**: what you count, such as "Tickets sold".
- **From**: where you start. Usually 0.
- **Target**: where you want to be. It can be lower than where you start, for something you want less of: waste from 500 kg down to 300 kg.
- **Now**: where you are today.
- **Unit**: optional, such as tickets, kg, € or %.

### Updating progress

Progress is updated by hand. Type the new number in the box on a key result and press **Update**. Weft shows who updated it last and when, and everyone looking at the set sees the change at once.

- A key result's progress is how far it has come from **From** towards **Target**, between 0 % and 100 %. Going past the target counts as 100 %.
- A goal's progress is the average of its key results. When it reaches 100 % it is marked **Reached**.
- **Overall** is the average of the goals that have key results.

Press **Edit** or **Delete** on a key result, or **Edit goal** or **Delete goal** on a goal, to change or remove it. Deleting a goal deletes its key results. Rename, move, share or delete a whole set the same way as a document; moving it to another channel moves all its goals with it.

### Finding goals

- The search box at the top finds goal sets by the words in their goals and key results, and Weft can answer questions such as "how many tickets have we sold?" from them.
- The **Goal progress** widget on Home lists the goal sets you can see with their overall progress. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the set's space can see its goals and progress; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners add, change and delete goals and key results and update the numbers. Viewers can follow the progress but not change anything. Weft can read goal sets to answer questions, but it cannot change them.
