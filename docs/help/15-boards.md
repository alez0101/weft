---
title: Boards
parent: Weft help
summary: A task list as columns (To do, Doing, Done, or your own), with cards you drag from one to the next.
---
**Boards** shows a task list as a board of columns, one card per task. It is not a separate list: the cards are the same tasks you see in **Tasks**, on the **Calendar** and in the **Tasks due soon** widget. Add or change a task in one place and it changes everywhere.

### Opening a board

Open **Boards** on Home and pick a task list on the left, or press **New task list** to make one (choose its space first). In **Tasks**, the **See as a board** button opens the list you are looking at as a board; on a board, **See as a list** goes back.

### Columns

- Every list starts with **To do**, **Doing** and **Done**.
- **Done** is always the last column. A card in Done is a ticked-off task, so ticking a task in Tasks moves its card to Done, and dragging a card to Done ticks it off. Dragging it back out reopens it.
- Editors can add a column with **New column** (it goes before Done, up to 10 besides Done), rename one by clicking its name, move it with **‹** and **›**, and delete it with **×**. Cards in a deleted column move to the first column.

### Cards

- Type in **Add a card** at the bottom of a column and press **Add**.
- Drag a card to another column, or up and down within one. With the keyboard, focus a card (Tab) and use the arrow keys: left and right move it between columns, up and down change its place.
- A card shows who it is assigned to and when it is due; overdue cards are marked in red. Change those, the title, or delete a task in **Tasks**.

### Who can see what

A board belongs to its list's space. Everyone in that space can see it; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners of the space can add cards, move them and change the columns. Viewers see the board but cannot move anything, except that the person a card is assigned to can press **Mark done** (or **Reopen**) on it. Weft can tell you which column a task is in, but it cannot change boards.
