---
title: Due-back dates
parent: Weft help
summary: Give a borrowed thing in Inventory a "back by" date; overdue things show in red, and Things you have lists the soonest first.
---
When a thing in **Inventory** is checked out, it can carry a **back by** date: the day it should come back. Once that day has passed, the thing says **Overdue** in red, so everyone can see what is late and who has it.

### Setting a date

- When you check a thing out, the form has a **Back by** box next to who is taking it. Pick a day, or leave it empty for no date.
- On a thing that is already out, press **Set back by date** (or **Change date** if it has one), pick a day and press **Save date**. **No date** removes it.
- The date cannot be in the past or more than five years ahead. A date that has already passed stays as it is until someone changes it or checks the thing in.
- Checking a thing in clears its date.

### Seeing what is due

- Each thing that is out says when it is due back, such as "back by Fri, 20 Nov". Overdue things say "was due back ..., overdue" in red and have an **Overdue** label.
- The **Overdue** button above the list shows only overdue things, and the count beside it says how many there are.
- **Checked out now** lists overdue things first, then the rest by date, then things with no date.
- The **Things you have** widget on Home lists what you have with the soonest date first, overdue ones in red.
- Weft can answer questions such as "when is the ladder due back?".

### Who can do what

Everyone in the inventory's space sees the dates; nobody else does. Editors and owners set, change and remove the date on anything that is out. If you are a viewer, you can set or change the date only on a thing you have (check it out to yourself, then press **Set back by date**). Weft can read the dates to answer questions, but it cannot change them.
