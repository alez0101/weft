"Who has a van licence?" "Does anyone in Volunteers know first aid?" **Who knows what** answers this from the profiles of the people you share a channel with. It is part of **Profiles**.

### Finding who knows something

1. Open **Profiles** on Home.
2. Under **People you share a space with**, the **Who knows what** row lists every skill people have put in their profile, with how many people have it. The most common come first.
3. Press a skill, such as **van licence**. The list shows only the people who have it, and the count above says, for example, **2 people know van licence**. Press it again to show everyone.

To look in one channel only, pick it in **Channel** (for example **#Volunteers**). The skill counts, the list and the people without a profile then include only that channel's members. **Any channel** shows everyone again. The skill, the channel and the find box work together.

If you pick a skill that no one in the chosen channel has, Weft says so.

You are not counted yourself: the row is about the others. Your own skills are in your profile at the top.

### On Home

Add the **Who knows what** widget with **Customize**. It shows the skills most people have. Press one to open Profiles showing who has it.

### Who can see what

Exactly what Profiles shows: the skills of people who share at least one channel or direct message with you. **Channel** lists only the channels you are in, so you cannot look into a channel you are not part of, not even to see a count. When someone leaves your last shared channel, or clears their profile, their skills disappear from the counts straight away.

Everyone sees this the same way, viewers too. Nobody can change someone else's skills: each person writes their own profile (see People profiles). Skills come only from what people type, so someone who has not filled in a profile is not counted.
