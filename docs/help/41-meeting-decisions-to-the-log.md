---
title: Meeting decisions to the log
parent: Weft help
summary: Send a line from a meeting's Decisions section to a decision log with one press, linked back to the meeting.
---
What a meeting decides should not stay buried in its notes. Each line you write under **Decisions** in a meeting can be sent to a decision log in the same channel with one press, so it is in the log with everything else the channel has decided, and the log says which meeting it came from.

### How to use it

- In **Meetings**, write what was decided under **Decisions**, one decision per line. When you leave the box, each line shows under it with a **Send to log** button.
- If the channel has more than one decision log, choose one in **Send to the log**. Next time it starts on the log you last used for this meeting. If the channel has no decision log yet, make one in **Decisions** first.
- Press **Send to log** next to a line. It becomes a decision in that log, dated the day the meeting was held, with the meeting's name under **Decided by**. The line now says **In** and the log's name; press **Open the log** to go there.
- In the decision log, the decision says **From the meeting** and the meeting's name. Press the name to open the meeting. You can edit the decision there like any other (add context, say what it replaces).
- Each line is sent once. If you delete the decision from the log, the meeting offers **Send to log** for that line again. Changing a line in the meeting after sending it does not change the decision; the changed line can be sent as a new one.
- Everyone with the meeting open sees a line marked as sent straight away.

### Who can see what

The meeting and the log are in the same channel, so the same people see both: everyone in that channel, viewers included, and nobody else, not in search and not in Weft's answers.

- Editors and owners can send lines. Viewers see which lines are in a log (and can open it), but cannot send them.
- If a meeting or a decision log is moved to another channel, the link between them is removed (the decision stays in its log). Nothing about it shows in the new channel.
- Deleting a meeting keeps the decisions it sent in their log.
- Weft (the assistant) can read the decisions and where they came from, for people who can see them. It cannot send lines.
