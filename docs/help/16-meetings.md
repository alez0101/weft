**Meetings** keeps everything about one meeting on one page: when it is, the agenda, the notes, what was decided, and the action items. Action items are real tasks in a task list you choose, so they also show in **Tasks**, **Boards**, on the **Calendar** and in the **Tasks due soon** widget, and people tick them off there or here.

### Starting a meeting

Open **Meetings** on Home, choose the space at the top (a channel, or your personal space) and press **New meeting**. Give it a name at the top. Or open an event in **Calendar** and press **Start meeting notes**: the meeting gets the event's name, time and space, and is linked to it.

### Writing it up

- **When**: the date and time of the meeting.
- **Calendar event**: pick an event from the same space. The meeting takes the event's time, **Open in Calendar** jumps to it, and the event shows a **Meeting notes** button that opens this page.
- **Agenda**, **Notes** and **Decisions**: plain text. Each one saves when you click or tab out of it, and everyone looking at the meeting sees it change. Two people can write in different sections at the same time; if two people change the same section, the last one to leave it wins.

### Action items

Under **Action items**, choose the task list they go to (**Add them to**). It must be in the same space as the meeting; make one in **Tasks** first if there is none. Then type the action item, pick who does it and when it is due, and press **Add action item**. It becomes a task at the bottom of that list.

**Remove** takes an item off the meeting but keeps the task in its list. Deleting a task in Tasks removes it from the meeting too. Deleting a meeting keeps its tasks.

### Who can see what

A meeting belongs to its space. Everyone in that space can read it, viewers included; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners change it, link it and add action items. Viewers read it, and can tick off an action item assigned to them. Weft (the assistant) can answer from a meeting's agenda, notes, decisions and action items for anyone who can open it, but cannot change meetings.

If a meeting, its task list or its event moves to another space, the links between them are removed, so nothing from one space shows in another.
