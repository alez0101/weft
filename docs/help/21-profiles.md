**Profiles** answers "who is this, what are they good at, and how do I reach them?" Each person can fill in one short profile about themselves. Everyone you share a channel or direct message with can read it.

### Filling in your profile

Open **Profiles** on Home and press **Fill in your profile**. Every field is optional:

- **Role**: what you do here, such as "Stage manager" or "Volunteer lead".
- **Skills**: up to 12, separated by commas, such as `first aid, van licence, Spanish`. Weft saves them in lower case and drops repeats.
- **How to reach me**: for example "Message me in Weft; call the site phone on the day".
- **When I'm available**: for example "Weekday evenings and all festival weekend".

Press **Save profile**. Press **Edit profile** to change it, or **Clear** to remove it (people then see only your name). If Weft cannot save something (more than 12 skills, for example), it says why above your profile.

The **Your profile** widget on Home shows your profile, or reminds you to fill it in. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.

### Finding someone

- Below your own profile are the profiles of the people you share a space with, with the channels you share shown on each one.
- Type in the find box to show only people whose name, role, skills or notes contain those words.
- Press a skill on any profile to show only people with that skill. Press it again to show everyone.
- People who have not filled in a profile are listed by name at the bottom.

### Who can see what

Your profile is seen by you and by anyone who shares at least one space with you: a channel (including the help channel, which every account is in), a direct message. When you leave a channel, its members who share nothing else with you stop seeing your profile.

Only you can change or clear your profile. Channel owners, Weft admins and Weft itself cannot. Weft can read profiles of people you share a space with, and those profiles appear in your search (search for a skill to find who has it). Someone you share no space with is never found.

Do not put anything in your profile you would not want everyone in your channels to see, such as a home address.
