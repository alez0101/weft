Every task has its own short comment thread. Use it for the questions and updates that belong to one task ("Which size stage?", "Banners are in the shed") so they stay with the task, and anyone who opens it later can read the whole story.

### How to use it

- In **Tasks**, press **Comment** under a task (or **2 comments** once there are some) to open its thread. Type in the box and press **Post**, or Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac).
- In **Boards**, every card shows the same button. Press it to read and add comments right on the card.
- Comments appear for everyone looking at the list or board straight away, oldest first, with who wrote them and when.
- Comments cannot be edited. To correct one, delete it and post again. **Delete** is shown next to your own comments, and to owners of the space next to every comment.
- On Home, the **New comments on your tasks** widget lists the latest comments other people left on tasks assigned to you. Press one to open its list. Add it with **Customize** if you do not see it.

### Who can see what

Comments belong to the task's list, so they follow the list's space. Everyone in that space can read them, viewers included; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers.

- Editors and owners can comment on any task. Viewers can comment only on a task assigned to them, the same way they can tick it off.
- You can delete your own comments. Owners of the space can delete any comment.
- Deleting a task deletes its comments.
- If a list is moved to another space, its comments go with it. A comment written by someone who is not in the new space is kept, but shows "Someone not in this space" instead of their name.
- Weft (the assistant) can read comments and use them in answers, for people who can see the list. It cannot post or delete them.
