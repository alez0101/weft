---
title: Your own agent
parent: Weft help
summary: Talking to your own AI agent (such as a Hermes agent) in Weft, in as many conversations as you like.
---
If you have your own AI agent (for example a Hermes agent you already chat with on your phone), a Weft admin can bring it into Weft for you. It then shows up in your chat list, under its name, and you can talk to it right here.

### Conversations

- Press **+** next to your agent's name to start a conversation. Start as many as you like: one for a packing list, another for a letter, another for planning a trip. Each one is its own thread, so the agent keeps them apart.
- A new conversation is named after your first message. To change the name, choose **Rename** under the title. **Delete** removes the conversation and its messages from Weft.
- Your agent can take a while on a bigger job. While it works, a line under the messages says so, and you can switch to another conversation (or start a new one) in the meantime. Its answer appears as soon as it is ready, even if you are looking at something else.
- Only you can see your conversations with your agent. Admins cannot open them.

### In channels

Mention your agent by its @name in a channel or direct message you are in (the message box shows the @name). It is sent that channel's recent messages so it knows what you are talking about, and nothing from anywhere else. It answers in your **Personal** space (at the top of the chat list), where only you can see it, starting with where you asked. If the answer is worth sharing, copy it into the channel yourself.

If a channel's owners have turned off **Allow AI agents to read this channel**, your agent cannot be asked there.

Only you can ask your own agent anything. If someone else mentions it, nothing happens.

### What your agent can see

Your agent sees only what is sent to it: your messages in a conversation, or in a channel the recent messages there. It cannot open your documents, tasks or calendar in Weft. Anything it remembers between conversations it keeps on its own side, the same as when you talk to it elsewhere.

If your agent cannot answer (it is switched off, or it is down), a short note says so instead of an answer. Try again in a moment, or ask your admin.

### Letting your agent use Weft

An admin can also give your agent **Weft access** (Settings → Personal agents). With it, your agent can search
and read what you can see in Weft, and create or change documents, tasks and events in your **Personal**
space, the same way a connected app like Claude does. It never deletes anything, and it cannot write in
channels; you move what it makes into a channel yourself. It appears under **Connected apps** in your account
menu, where you can disconnect it at any time.
