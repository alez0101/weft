title: Spreadsheets
parent: Weft help
summary: Real spreadsheets with formulas, formatting and charts, edited together in Weft, and Excel or CSV files opened as spreadsheets.
---
**Spreadsheets** is a full spreadsheet in Weft: formulas, number formats, sorting and filters, charts, several sheets in one file. Everyone in the sheet sees each other's changes as they type. It is different from **Tables**, which keeps simple lists with typed columns.

### Making one

- In **Spreadsheets**, pick a space and press **New spreadsheet**.
- Or press **open a file as a spreadsheet** under it and pick an Excel (.xlsx or .xls), OpenDocument (.ods), CSV or TSV file. Weft makes a spreadsheet from it.
- In **Files**, an Excel or CSV file has an **Open as spreadsheet** button that does the same, in the file's space. The original file stays as it was.

### Working in it

- Rename, move or delete a spreadsheet from the bar above it, like a document.
- Changes save by themselves while you work, and again when everyone has closed it. There is no Save button to remember.
- **File, Download as** saves a copy as .xlsx, .ods, .csv or PDF.
- If Weft was just restarted, the spreadsheet may take about a minute to open; the page says so and opens it when it is ready.

### Who can do what

- People who can edit the space can change the spreadsheet. Viewers see it, read-only, with other people's changes as they happen, and can download or print it.
- Search and Weft's answers can use what is in the cells (dates as dates), for anyone who can open the spreadsheet.
