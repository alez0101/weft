---
title: Repeating tasks
parent: Weft help
summary: A task that comes back every day, week or month: tick it off and the next one appears.
---
A **repeating task** is a task in **Tasks** that comes back every day, every week or every month. Water the plants, take out the recycling, pay the rent: enter it once, and each time someone ticks it off, the next one appears in the same list.

### Making a task repeat

In a task list, the form for a new task has a **Repeats** choice next to who and when: Does not repeat, Every day, Every week or Every month. For a task that is already on the list, change its **Repeats** choice in its row.

### Ticking it off

Tick the box as usual. The task moves to **Done**, and a new one with the same name, the same person and the same repeat appears among the open tasks, due on the next date of its schedule after today:

- A weekly task due on a Monday comes back due the next Monday, also when it was ticked off late, so it stays on Mondays.
- A monthly task due on the 31st comes back on the last day of a shorter month, and goes on from that day.
- A repeating task with no due date comes back due a day, a week or a month from today.

The task you ticked off stays done and stops repeating, so reopening it and ticking it off again does not make a second copy. To stop a task repeating, set its **Repeats** back to Does not repeat. If the person it was assigned to has left the channel, the next one is assigned to no one.

Boards and the **Tasks due soon** widget on Home also say how a task repeats, and dragging a repeating card to Done on a board brings the next one back the same way.

### Who can see what

The same as any task: everyone in the list's space sees its tasks and how they repeat, viewers included; nobody else does. Editors and owners make tasks repeat or stop them repeating. Someone who can only read the list may still tick off a task assigned to them, and so cause the next one, but cannot change how it repeats. Weft (the assistant) can read how a task repeats but does not change tasks.
