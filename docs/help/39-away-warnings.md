summary: A gentle "Pat is away then" when you pick someone for a task, a shift or a meeting action on a day they have time off.
---
When you give someone a task, see them on a shift or make them the owner of a meeting action on a day their **time off** covers, Weft shows a small note next to their name: "Pat is away then". If it is you, it says "You are away then". It is only a reminder: you can still choose them.

### Where you see it

- **Tasks**: in the form to add a task, as soon as you pick the person and a due date, and on every open task whose due date falls in the assignee's time off.
- **Meetings**: on action items the same way. An action item without a due date uses the day the meeting is held.
- **Shifts**: by the name of anyone on a shift during their time off, and above **Take a place** if you are away that day yourself.
- **Home**: the **Tasks due soon** widget adds "away then" after the person's name.

The note follows the time off. Change the dates in **Time off**, or remove them, and the note appears or goes at once, for everyone looking. A task with no due date, a done task and a shift that has ended get no note.

### Who can see what

The note comes only from time off you can already see: time off in channels you are a member of, viewers included. If Pat recorded a holiday only in a channel you are not in, you get no note about it, and the note never shows the reason or the channel. Nothing new is stored, so there is nothing to change or delete here; Weft (the assistant) does not use these notes.
