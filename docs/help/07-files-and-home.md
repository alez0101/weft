### Files

Open **Files** on Home. Choose the space at the top (your personal space, or a channel you can edit), then press **Upload files** or drop files onto the list.

- Everyone in that space can open and download the file; nobody else can see it, not even in search or in Weft's answers.
- **PDFs, Word documents (.docx) and text files** (.txt, .md, .csv, .json and similar) have their text read, so you can search for them and ask Weft about them. Weft answers from a file the same way it answers from a document, with a link back to it.
- Pictures and other kinds of files are stored and can be downloaded, but Weft does not read them. Scanned PDFs (pictures of pages) have no text to read.
- PDFs and pictures show a preview; press **Download** to save any file.
- Files can be up to 25 MB. Rename, move, share or delete a file the same way as a document.

### Your Home widgets

Below the apps, Home shows widgets: **Announcements** (current notices pinned to your channels), **Coming up** (the next two weeks of your calendars), **Your upcoming yeses** (events you said Going to that have not ended), **Your bookings** (things you booked, until the booking ends), **Your shifts** (shifts you are on, until they end), **Unread** (conversations with new messages), **Tasks due soon** (open tasks that are overdue or due within a week), **Polls to vote in** (open polls you have not voted in yet), **Forms to fill in** (open forms you have not answered yet), **New links** (the newest links in lists you can see; not on by default, add it with **Customize**), **New contacts** (the newest contacts in address books you can see; not on by default), **Recent decisions** (the most recently decided decisions in logs you can see; not on by default), **Your profile** (your own profile in Profiles, or a reminder to fill it in; not on by default), **Recent meetings** (the latest meetings you can open, by date; not on by default), **Recently changed**, **Recent files** and **Ask Weft** (a quick question for your Weft conversation). Every widget shows only what you can open.

Press **Customize** to choose which widgets you see and to put them in your order. Your choice follows you to any browser you sign in on.
