title: Kudos on profiles

summary: See the newest thank-yous someone received on their card in Profiles, only from channels you are in too.
---
When you look someone up in **Profiles**, their card also shows the kudos they received lately: what they were thanked for, by whom, in which channel and when. It is part of **Profiles**; kudos are still given in **Kudos**.

### What you see

- Each card shows up to three of the person's newest kudos, newest first, and how many kudos you can see for them in all.
- Your own card shows **Kudos you received**.
- Press a channel name, such as **#festival**, to open that channel's kudos wall.
- Kudos show on the cards of people who have filled in a profile. Your own kudos show even if you have not filled in yours.
- A new or deleted kudos shows up at once, without reloading.

### Who can see what

You see only kudos from channels you are in yourself. A kudos for Olive in Organizers is not shown to someone who is not in Organizers: it is not listed, it does not push another kudos out of the newest three, and it is not in the count. So two people can see different kudos on the same card. If you leave a channel, its kudos disappear from the cards you see. A link scoped to one channel (for an AI app) sees only that channel's kudos.

Nobody can give, change or delete a kudos from Profiles; that stays in **Kudos**, under its usual rules. Weft (the assistant) reads the same kudos you do and cannot change them.
