title: Repeating bookings
parent: Weft help
summary: Book a shared thing at the same time every week for a number of weeks; weeks that clash are skipped and Weft tells you which.
---
In **Bookings**, a booking can repeat **every week**: the same day of the week and the same time, for 2 to 26 weeks. Use it for a weekly rehearsal, a standing team meeting or the van every Saturday morning.

### Booking every week

1. Pick the thing on the left and press **Book**.
2. Choose the first day, the start and the end, as for a single booking.
3. Under **Repeat**, choose **Every week** and say for how many weeks, counting the first one.
4. Press **Book 4 weeks** (the button says how many).

If some of those weeks are already taken by someone else's booking, Weft books all the others and tells you which dates it could not book, and whose booking is in the way, for example "Booked 3 of 4 weeks. Not booked, because the time is already taken: Mon 30 Nov, 18:00 to 19:30 (booked by Alex)". If every week is taken, nothing is booked and Weft lists the dates.

The time stays the same on the clock all through, also when the clocks change for summer or winter time. A weekly booking can last up to a week each time.

### Seeing and cancelling

Each week is an ordinary booking: it shows in **Coming up** with a **Weekly** label, on the Calendar and in the **Your bookings** widget. You can change or cancel one week as usual. **Cancel this and later weeks** gives back that week and every later week of the same repeat; earlier weeks, and weeks that have already ended, stay.

### Who can do what

The same as for any booking: everyone in the channel sees the weekly bookings, with names; nobody else can. Editors and owners can book every week; viewers cannot. You cancel your own weeks; an owner of the channel can cancel anyone's. Weft (the assistant) can read them but cannot book or cancel anything.
