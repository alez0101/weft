---
**Contacts** keeps the people a group needs to reach in one place: the caterer, the venue manager, the first aid lead, the council's events office. An address book belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and only the people in that space can see it.

### Making an address book and adding people

Open **Contacts** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New address book**. Type a name over "Contacts", such as "Suppliers" or "Volunteers". A channel can have more than one.

Press **Add a contact** and fill in:

- **Name**: the only thing you have to fill in.
- **Organisation**: the company, group or role, such as "Stage crew".
- **Phone**: digits, spaces and `+ - ( ) . / x` only.
- **Email**: one plain address, such as `dana@example.org`. Weft saves it in lower case.
- **Notes**: anything that helps, such as when to call or what they can help with.

If Weft cannot save something (an email that is not a plain address, for example), it says why above the list.

Press **Edit** or **Delete** on a contact to change or remove it. Rename, move, share or delete a whole address book the same way as a document. Moving it to another channel moves all its contacts with it.

### Finding someone

- Contacts are listed by name.
- Type in the find box to show only contacts whose name, organisation, phone, email or notes contain those words. Phone numbers match however they are spaced: `900789` finds `07700 900789`.
- Click a phone number to call it, or an email address to write to it, with your own phone or email app.
- The search box at the top finds address books by the words in their contacts, and Weft can answer questions such as "what is the caterer's number?" from them.
- The **New contacts** widget on Home lists the newest contacts in address books you can see. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the address book's space can see its contacts; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners add, change and delete contacts. Viewers can read them, call and email, but not change anything. Weft can read address books to answer questions, but it cannot add, change or delete contacts.

Contact details are about real people outside Weft. Keep only what the group needs, and delete people who no longer need to be there.
