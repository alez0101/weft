---
title: Settle up in one press
parent: Weft help
summary: In Expenses, press Record payment next to a suggested payment once the money has changed hands, and Weft writes the pay-back down for you.
---
In an expense list, **To settle up** suggests who should pay whom to make everyone even, such as "Pat pays Olive 22.50". Once Pat has actually paid Olive (in cash, by bank transfer, however you like), press **Record payment** next to that line. Weft asks you to confirm, then writes it down. No money moves through Weft; this only saves typing the pay-back in by hand. It is part of **Expenses**; there is nothing to switch on.

### What it writes

- A new expense called "Pat paid Olive back", for the full amount on the line, paid by Pat, split by amounts with all of it to Olive, dated today, added by you.
- Everyone's balances and the **To settle up** list change at once, for everyone looking. Pat's line disappears.
- It is an ordinary expense: **Edit** or **Delete** it like any other. Deleting it brings the suggestion back.

### If it says no

- If the balances changed since you looked (someone else just recorded the same payment, or changed an expense), Weft refuses rather than write a payment twice, and says how much is owed now. The list reloads with the new suggestions.
- If the person paying or being paid has left the channel, there is no button on that line: add the payment by hand if you still need it.
- For a part payment, or a payment that is not on the list, add it as an expense by hand (see Expenses).

### Who can do this

Editors and owners of the list's channel see **Record payment** and can use it. Viewers see the suggestions but no button. Like every expense, the payment is seen only by people in the channel, and Weft (the assistant) can read it but cannot record payments.
