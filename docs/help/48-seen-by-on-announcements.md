---
title: Seen by on announcements
parent: Weft help
summary: Ask everyone in a channel to press Got it on an announcement, and see how many have.
---
Some notices must not be missed: the wristbands change on Friday, the fire exit has moved, the meeting point is different. When you post such an announcement you can ask everyone in the channel to press **Got it**. You then see how many have pressed it, and who has not yet, so you know whom to remind.

### Asking for Got it

1. In **Announcements**, press **New announcement** (or **Edit** on a notice that is already up).
2. Tick **Ask everyone to press “Got it”** and press **Post** (or **Save**).

Under the notice you now see, for example, **1 of 3 members got it** and **Not yet:** followed by the names of the others. The count is out of everyone in the channel except whoever posted the notice, and it moves as people press the button. When everyone has pressed it, it says **Everyone has seen it**.

### Pressing Got it

A notice that asks shows a **Got it** button for everyone in the channel, viewers included: at the top of the channel's chat, in the **Announcements** widget on Home and in the app. Press it once you have read the notice; it turns into **You got it**. Pressed by mistake? **Undo** takes it back.

You can press it only while the notice is showing. When a notice ends, the answers already given stay, so the people who posted it can still see who had seen it.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the channel sees the button and their own answer, nobody else's. Only the editors and owners of the channel (the people who post announcements) see how many have pressed it and who has not. Nobody outside the channel sees anything. If you leave the channel, your answers there go. Weft (the assistant) does not read who pressed Got it.
