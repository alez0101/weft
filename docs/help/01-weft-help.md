Weft puts chat, documents, a wiki, whiteboards, tables, task lists, polls, forms, link lists, shared address books, decision logs, Q and A boards, reusable checklists, shared expenses, meeting notes, announcements, bookings of shared things, shift sign-ups and a calendar with RSVPs in one place, with an assistant that answers from what you can see.

### Finding your way around

- **Chat** is on the left: your conversation with Weft at the top, then channels, then direct messages. Use the arrows at the top of the chat rail to collapse it to icons or widen it.
- **Home** is always the first tab. Click an app to open it in its own tab; click it again later and Weft switches to the tab you already have open. Below the apps are your widgets; **Customize** chooses them.
- **Search**: the box at the top searches everything you can open: channels, documents, wiki pages, tables, task lists, polls, forms, link lists, meetings, whiteboards, files, events and messages. Press **/** (or Ctrl+K) to jump to it, use the arrow keys and Enter to open a result. Words match as you type; **Related by meaning** lists things that match the idea rather than the exact words.
- **Appearance**: the menu next to your initial switches between System, Light and Dark.
