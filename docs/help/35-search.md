The **search box** at the top finds things across Weft: messages, documents and the other items, events, channels, and the rows of the apps that are not documents: announcements, who is going to an event, things to book (with their bookings), shifts (with who is on them), time off, kudos, timeline milestones, profiles and your own journal.

### Using it

Click the box, or press **/** (or Ctrl+K, Cmd+K on a Mac), and type. Words match as you type. Results are grouped by kind (**Announcements**, **Shifts**, **Kudos**, **Your journal** and so on), with the matching words highlighted. Use the arrow keys and **Enter**, or click a result, to open it in its app: an announcement opens the announcements of its channel, a kudos the channel's wall, a journal page that day in Journal, a profile the Profiles app.

Below the word matches, **Related by meaning** lists things about the same topic in other words (if your Weft has meaning search turned on).

Weft (the assistant) uses the same things when you ask it a question, and links to where each answer came from.

### Who can see what

Search shows only what you could open in the app itself, and nothing is copied anywhere to make it searchable:

- Rows of a channel's apps (announcements, bookings, shifts, time off, kudos, milestones, answers to its events): only for the people in that channel.
- Profiles: only of people you share a space with.
- Journal pages: only for their writer. Weft uses them only in your own Weft conversation, never when you ask in a channel, a direct message or your personal space, and AI apps you connect to Weft never find them.

When you ask Weft in a channel, it uses only that channel, because everyone there reads the answer.
