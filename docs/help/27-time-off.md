---
title: Time off
parent: Weft help
summary: Say when you are away (holiday, sick, a few days off) and see who in your channels is out this week.
---
**Time off** answers "who is away when?" for a channel: Olive is on holiday Monday to Friday, Vic is off sick today. You add your own days off; everyone in the channel sees them, on the Calendar and on Home too.

### Seeing who is away

Open **Time off** on Home. At the top is a week, Monday to Sunday, with a row for each person away that week and a bar on the days they are out (today is shaded). Use **‹** and **›** for other weeks and **This week** to come back. Below it, **Now and coming up** lists every entry that has not ended yet, soonest first, with the days, the channel and the note.

On the left you can pick one channel, or **All channels**.

### Adding your time off

Press **Add time off**. Choose the channel, the first and last day you are away (both days count; one day off is the same day twice) and, if you like, a short note: "holiday", "at a conference", "sick". Press **Add time off** again. An entry can be up to 366 days; for longer, add another.

Press **Change** on your entry to move the days or change the note, or **Remove** to take it away. If you are away from more than one channel, add it in each channel that should know.

Editors and owners of a channel can also add time off for anyone in that channel (pick them under **Who**), for example when someone phones in sick, and can change or remove anyone's entry there.

### On the Calendar and Home

The **Calendar** shows time off as all-day "Away" entries in the channel's colour. The **Time off** switch on the left hides them. Click one to open Time off.

The **Out this week** widget on Home lists everyone away at some point this week in your channels, with their days and note. If you chose your widgets before this widget existed, add it with **Customize**.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the channel sees its time off, with names and notes; nobody else can. Every member adds, changes and removes their own, viewers included. Editors and owners can do that for anyone in the channel. Time off never moves to another channel. If you leave a channel, or are removed from it, your time off there is removed. Deleting a channel deletes its time off. Weft (the assistant) can read time off but cannot add, change or remove it, and time off is not in search.
