**Checklists** are for the jobs you do the same way every time: opening up the site, packing the van, welcoming a new volunteer. You write the steps down once. Each time you do the job you start a **run**, a fresh copy of the steps that you tick through, and when you are done it stays as a record of who did what and when. A checklist belongs to a space (a channel, or your personal space), and only the people in that space can see it.

### Making a checklist

Open **Checklists** on Home. Choose the space at the top and press **New checklist**. Type a name over "New checklist", such as "Opening up".

Under **Steps**, type a step and press **Add step**, one at a time. Use the arrows to put them in order, **Rename** to change one and **Remove** to take one out. Rename, move, share or delete a whole checklist the same way as a document.

### Doing a run

Press **Start a run**. You can give it a name first, such as "Saturday", or leave it blank. The run appears under **Runs** with every step unticked. Tick each step as it is done; Weft shows who ticked it and when, and everyone looking at the checklist sees the ticks straight away, so two people can work through one run together. More than one run can be in progress at a time.

When you are done, press **Finish**. The run moves to **Past runs**, with who finished it and when, and its ticks can no longer be changed. Press **Reopen** if it was finished too early. **Delete run** removes a run and its ticks for good.

Changing the steps never changes a run that has already started: the run keeps the steps it started with. The next run uses the steps as they are then. A checklist needs at least one step before it can be run, and can have up to 100.

### Finding things

- The **Checklists in progress** widget on Home lists runs not finished yet, with how far each has got. It is not on by default: add it with **Customize**.
- The search box at the top finds checklists by the words in their steps, and Weft can answer questions such as "what's on the opening up checklist?" or "who finished Saturday's opening?" from them.

### Who can see what

Everyone in the checklist's space can see its steps, its runs and who ticked what; nobody else can, not in search and not in Weft's answers. Editors and owners change the steps, start runs, tick steps, finish, reopen and delete runs. Viewers can read everything but not change it. Weft can read checklists to answer questions, but it cannot tick, start or change anything.
