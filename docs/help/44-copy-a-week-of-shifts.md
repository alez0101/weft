In **Shifts**, editors and owners of a channel can copy a whole day or a whole week of shifts to a later date in one go. Use it when the rota is the same every week: set it up once, then copy it on.

### Copying a week

1. Open **Shifts** and press **Copy shifts** (next to **New shift**).
2. Choose the channel, then **a week**, and the first day of the week to copy. The week is that day and the six after it.
3. **To** is the day the copy starts on. It is set to a week later; change it to copy further on.
4. Weft lists the shifts it will copy and the day each one lands on. Press **Copy 5 shifts** (the button says how many).

To copy one day, choose **a day**, or press **Copy this day** next to a day's heading in the list.

### What is copied

Each copy is an ordinary shift with the same name, times, number of places and note, the same number of days later. Times stay the same on the clock, also when the clocks change for summer or winter time. **Nobody is signed up on a copy**: people take places as usual.

Shifts that have already ended can be copied too, so last week's rota can become next week's. If a shift is already there at the new time (same name, start and end), it is not made twice; Weft tells you how many were already there. A copy that would already be over is not made. Up to 300 shifts can be copied at once, up to a year ahead.

Copies can be changed or deleted one by one like any shift.

### Who can do what

Only editors and owners of the channel can copy its shifts, and only within that channel. Everyone in the channel sees the copies, viewers included; nobody else can. Weft (the assistant) cannot copy shifts.
